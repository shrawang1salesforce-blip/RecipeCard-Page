Dinner · serves 4

Lemon herb chicken

Golden chicken thighs, bright lemon, and a handful of herbs make this an easy dinner worth repeating.

10 min prep 30 min cook Easy level

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs, boneless4
  • Garlic cloves, crushed3
  • Lemon, zested and juiced1
  • Fresh rosemary2 sprigs
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Salt and black pepperto taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Pat the chicken dry, then season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Warm the oil in an ovenproof pan. Place the chicken skin-side down and cook for 5 minutes, until golden.
  3. Turn the chicken over. Add the garlic, lemon zest, juice, and rosemary around the pan.
  4. Roast for 20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
✦

Good with warm bread, dressed greens, or whatever vegetables are waiting in the fridge.
